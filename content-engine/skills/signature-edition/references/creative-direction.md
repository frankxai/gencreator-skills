# Creative direction that changes the work

Write for one person facing one particular decision. Establish what they know, what they feel, what they risk and what the piece can honestly change.

A treatment is a sequence of actual choices. Name the opening image or claim, the evidence or event that develops it, the moment that changes its meaning and the ending's concrete invitation. Avoid a list of adjectives standing in for art direction.

Compare a direction faithful to the approved signature, a deliberate evolution, and a different organizing principle. Show a real paragraph, scene opening or spoken opening for each. Differences need to affect at least two of these:

- Composition: sequence, focal point, density, relationships and negative space.
- Language: sentence movement, narrative distance, point of view and level of abstraction.
- Material: paper, footage, illustration, typography, sound or another medium with a job.
- Interaction: what the reader controls, revisits or contributes.
- Motion: timing, transitions, camera and reduced-motion alternative.

A restrained editorial treatment, an intimate field recording and a navigable evidence study should produce different experiences even in the same colors. A static essay needs no animation. Music should change the scene's meaning before it is added.

Keep an anti-signature: the habits that would make this creator interchangeable. Use the creator's rejected examples when available. In their absence, mark the direction provisional. Common weak choices include grand claims before evidence, pseudo-confessions, generic hero language, copied influencer cadence and decorative technology.

For cross-brand work, retain separate audience promises, voice examples and canon. Reuse delivery mechanics. A shared workflow does not grant permission to copy a private brand corpus into another product.

Assess the finished work against an actual alternative on the same input when possible. Report the difference observed, repair time and uncertainty. A proposed advantage stays a hypothesis until evaluated.

End with the piece delivered, its editable source and one useful next action. A production plan alone does not satisfy a request for finished work.
