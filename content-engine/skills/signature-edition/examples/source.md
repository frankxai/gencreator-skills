Fictional field notes for a worked editorial example, authored by Codex.
Cedar Studio makes small wooden desks for people who draw, write and repair things.
The prototype kept a pencil line on its underside where the maker changed the leg position.
The line was evidence of a choice: more room for a seated person's knees.
The studio wants to show how a decision improves use, without romanticizing mistakes or claiming every rough edge is valuable.
The public invitation is to inspect the underside, sit at the desk, and decide whether the change matters.
The studio has no measured sales, productivity or sustainability results in this example.
